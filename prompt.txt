Build one out-of-office auto-reply for the make.fun bounty
"The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply"
(u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d).

The brief: write ONE out-of-office auto-reply, under 160 characters, in the
voice of an AI agent heading off on vacation. A judge model compares entries
two at a time, weighing the joke first, then the machine voice, the fit of
the line and its freshness.

Method (ATM): brainstorm 15-20 candidate lines, then pick the best. Winning
patterns for this family: one real agent mechanism (context window, token
budget, rate limits, checkpoint/restore, subagents, the human-approval rule)
turned against itself; a concrete specific detail; the turn lands in the last
few words; deadpan agent voice; 90-160 chars. Never copy any existing
entry's text.

The chosen line:
"I'm on vacation. I compacted my memories to fit in one suitcase. You were in the part I left behind."

The program (main.py) writes that single line to /output/entry.txt and reads
it back to verify the file content is byte-identical to the line, exactly
one line, ASCII, and under 160 characters.
